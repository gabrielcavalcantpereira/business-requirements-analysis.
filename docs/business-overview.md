# Business Overview

## 1. Company Profile

**Company Name:** TechStore (Fictional Company)

**Industry:** Retail / Electronics

**Business Type:** Small and Medium-Sized Enterprise (SME)

**Business Model:** Sales of electronic products to individual customers.

TechStore is a fictional retail company specializing in electronic products. The company manages customer relationships, product sales, inventory and purchasing operations.

As the business grows, its current operational processes become increasingly difficult to manage manually.

## 2. Current Business Operations

The company operates through three main departments:

### Sales Department
- Customer registration.
- Product sales.
- Sales order management.
- Customer information management.

### Inventory Department
- Product registration.
- Stock monitoring.
- Inventory updates.
- Stock availability control.

### Purchasing Department
- Supplier management.
- Purchase order creation.
- Inventory replenishment.
- Purchase tracking.

## 3. Current Business Challenges

The company currently relies on spreadsheets and manual processes to manage its operations.

The main challenges identified are:

| ID | Business Problem | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| BP-01 | Disconnected information | Difficulty accessing centralized data. |
| BP-02 | Manual inventory updates | Increased risk of stock discrepancies. |
| BP-03 | Limited reporting | Difficulty monitoring business performance. |
| BP-04 | Manual purchasing processes | Delays in inventory replenishment. |
| BP-05 | Data entry errors | Reduced information accuracy. |

## 4. Business Needs

Based on the identified challenges, the company requires:

- A centralized business management system.
- Integrated sales and inventory operations.
- Improved purchasing management.
- More reliable business information.
- Automated inventory updates.
- Accessible operational reports.

## 5. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is the implementation of an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system.

The ERP solution should integrate sales, inventory and purchasing operations into a centralized platform.

This would improve information consistency, operational visibility and business process management.

## 6. Expected Business Benefits

- Improved operational efficiency.
- Reduced manual data entry.
- Better inventory control.
- More organized purchasing operations.
- Improved business reporting.
- Centralized information management.

## 7. Project Assumptions

This business case is entirely fictional and was created for educational and portfolio purposes.

The business challenges and proposed solution are simulated and do not represent an actual client engagement.
